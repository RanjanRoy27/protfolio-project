FAQ

Plain answers before we talk.

Do I need AI?

Not always. Sometimes automation alone solves the problem. The audit starts by finding the simplest reliable fix.

Can you work with small businesses?

Yes. The best fit is an owner-operated or ops-heavy business with repetitive workflows that already cost time every week.

Do you build custom solutions?

Yes. I design around your tools, documents, team workflow, and business outcome instead of forcing a generic template.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on scope and complexity. Small automations can move quickly; larger RAG or multi-tool systems need more design and testing.

What happens during a discovery call?

We review the workflow, estimate the cost of the bottleneck, and decide whether automation is worth pursuing.
